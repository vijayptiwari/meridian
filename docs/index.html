A complete platform for modern job search

Meridian replaces fragmented tools with one disciplined workflow — from discovery through preparation, with the reliability professionals expect from production software.

01

Multi-agent orchestration

Dedicated agents for research, compensation, fit analysis, resume editing, and interview prep — each stage optimized for its task.

02

Local dashboard

A full web interface at 127.0.0.1:3030 to manage runs, review shortlists, export data, and configure settings.

03

Provider-neutral AI

Connect OpenAI, Azure, Groq, or a local Ollama instance. Switch providers without migrating your data or workflow.

04

Controlled automation

Browser automation runs locally with human oversight. You review every submission before it leaves your machine.

Career planning modes
