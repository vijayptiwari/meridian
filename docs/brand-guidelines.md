# Meridian brand guidelines

## Mark

Two elements: vertical line + champagne point (`#BFA56A`).

Minimum size: 20px. Clear space = height of mark on all sides.

## Colors

| Token | Hex |
|---|---|
| Accent | `#BFA56A` |
| Background | `#060B10` |
| Ink | `#F4F7FA` |

## Wordmark

Plus Jakarta Sans 500, sentence case: **Meridian**

## Tagline

*Align your next move.*

## Don't

- No gradients or glow on the logo
- No "AI-powered" badges stacked on the mark
- No login/signup flows — Meridian is local-first

Assets: `docs/brand/` and `src/ui/public/brand/`
