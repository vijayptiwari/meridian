How Meridian compares

A structured comparison against job boards, automation tools, and AI resume platforms — focused on privacy, control, and workflow completeness for professional users.

The problem with most tools

Job boards & aggregators

  • Show listings, not strategy
  • No resume tailoring or fit scoring
  • Your activity fuels their ad business

Auto-apply bots

  • Spray-and-pray applications damage your brand
  • Opaque automation risks ToS violations
  • No learning prep or career planning

AI resume SaaS

  • Upload your resume to someone else's cloud
  • Monthly fees for features you could run locally
  • Locked to one vendor's model and UI

Generic ChatGPT workflows

  • Manual copy-paste for every job
  • No portal integration or run history
  • No structured shortlist or export pipeline

Meridian's design principles

  1. Local-first by default — Browser sessions, resumes, run logs, and Gmail tokens live in your project folder. The UI binds to 127.0.0.1 unless you explicitly change it.
  2. Assisted, not reckless — Meridian helps you move faster; you review tailored resumes and confirm applications. You remain accountable to platform terms of service.
  3. Provider-neutral AI — Use OpenAI, Groq, Azure, Ollama on localhost, or your own webhook. Switch providers in config without migrating your data.
  4. Operator tooling — npm run doctor, demo mode, setup checklist, and structured JSON outputs are built for repeatability — not one-off chat sessions.
  5. Open source trust — Every agent, scorer, and portal adapter is in the repo. Fork it, patch selectors when portals change, add your own agents.
Summary: Meridian is a local career operations platform — not a listing aggregator or unsupervised automation tool. It is built for professionals who need transparency, data ownership, and repeatable workflows.
