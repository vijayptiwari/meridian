    Meridian

    Open-source, local-first career operations agent.

    Meridian helps you search roles, score fit, tailor resumes, plan upskilling, and explore career transitions — as a structured workflow on your own machine.

    • Your resume, run logs, and browser sessions stay on this device.
    • No Meridian account or cloud upload of your profile is required.
    • LinkedIn and Naukri runs are assisted workflows — you review outputs before applying.
    • LLM calls only leave your machine when you configure a provider.
